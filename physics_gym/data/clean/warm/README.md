# clean warm starts

Policies trained only on clean data (the CMU motion capture; no Bandai Namco clips), for continuing training.

| file | what |
|---|---|
| walk_cmu.tss / .head | **superseded: do not warm-start from it.** Trained on the CMU walk cycle as converted before the rest-direction fix in tools/cmu_to_ref.py, which crossed the legs (knees 0.27 m, feet 0.47 m past each other), and before leg-on-leg contact; it learned that crossed gait. Kept only as the record of the first clean run (unassisted, 64 x 20 s, no falls, 0.96 m/s). |
| walk_cmu2.tss / .head | **the clean walk prior.** Imitation of the corrected CMU walk cycle (legs no longer crossed), with leg-on-leg contact, CMU joint ranges and muscles: 90 min stage on an A40 from scratch, adaptive assist 0.5 -> 0 (this is the checkpoint at assist 0, about 70 min in). Unassisted, mean actions, 20 s on the open floor (drift stop off): 6% falls over 64 episodes (9% over 32), 0.98 m/s; knees 7.9 cm apart on average, crossed in 4% of steps (13% for the feet) against 31% and 53% for walk_cmu. |
| walk_cmu3.tss / .head | **the clean walk prior (current).** The same run as walk_cmu2, at its end (90 min, 81.5M steps, about 20 min of it unassisted). Unassisted, mean actions, 64 episodes of 20 s on the open floor (drift stop off): **no falls**, 0.98 m/s (0.97 forward); knees 7.9 cm apart on average, crossed in 1.7% of steps (feet 11%). |
| walk_robust_slick.tss / .head | walk_cmu3 trained on in free-heading imitation with pushes up to 80 N (40 min, gpu/pod_robust_terrain.sh), before static friction: 20 s, open floor, no falls with mean actions, 9% at sigma 0.14 (walk_cmu3: 98%). Under the static-friction physics (which grips where the old let feet slip) it lasts 10 s on average; the warm start for retraining on it. |
| walk_robust.tss / .head | walk_robust_slick retrained under static friction (free-heading imitation, pushes to 80 N, assist adapted to 0; 40 min, gpu/pod_robust_terrain.sh stage W): 20 s on open floor, no falls with mean actions, 5% at sigma 0.139, 70% at sigma 0.2. The viewer's Walk (CMU) from artifact v12. |
| walk_contact.tss / .head | walk_robust retrained after the contact fix (static friction per body against penetration depth, no bounce; gpu/pod_contact.sh stage W, 20 min, assist adapted to 0): 20 s open floor, no falls with mean actions, 2% at sigma 0.139. The viewer's Walk (CMU) from artifact v16. |
| goto_contact.tss / .head | go-to over the stage-1 room bank after the contact fix (gpu/pod_contact.sh stage A: from walk_contact, fresh goal/grip/probe inputs, goals ahead, 60% on raised surfaces; 40 min): 5.7% hand-up. The viewer's Go to from artifact v19; the unified run's warm start. |
| unified_3.tss / .head | the unified navigation run's last stage (gpu/pod_night.sh -> pod_unified.sh: 24 slope gyms + 8 stage-1 rooms, the path helper's waypoint as input and progress along the path as reward, 40 s of chained goals; 5 h). With its 5% hand-up: on the slope bank 74 goals in 228 s over 24 episodes (goto_contact: 34), but 19 of 24 fall within 15 s, a rushing gait (pelvis to 2.5 m/s) and hard foot strikes. Without the hand-up it falls within 2 s. The viewer's Go to from artifact v20. |
| getup_v2.tss / .head | get-up in the gym's physics (pod_night.sh step 1: CMU get-up clips as style, a hand-up fading 0.5 -> 0 over 40 min, then 20 min alone, entropy bonus): alone it reaches an upright kneel (pelvis 0.30 m, head 0.71 m) and stops; with a 20% hand-up it stands in about a second. The viewer's get-up (with that hand-up) from artifact v20. |
| getup_imit.tss / .head | imitation of the six CMU get-ups as a clip set (gpu/pod_getup_imitate.sh: task getup_imitate, each episode from a random frame of a clip, once through and then its last frame held; hand-up adapted to 0). Starting from a clip's own lying state, alone: 30 of 66 stand. From a real fall with the nearest frame taking over: 4 of 95. The warm start for getup_track. |
| getup_track.tss / .head | **the get-up tracker.** getup_imit trained on with 40% of episodes starting from a real fall (a bank of 1024 limp, shoved falls and all-fours settles; the nearest frame of the six get-ups by joint angles, pelvis tilt and height taking over as the reference), the hand-up adapted 0.3 -> 0 (gpu/pod_getup_track.sh, 60 min on an A40, 51M steps). Alone, no hand-up: from real falls **42 of 95** reach standing height (getup_imit 4); from clip starts 44 of 66 (getup_imit 30). In body.js, the viewer's own path (limp fall, nearestFrame, the clip playing on): 23 of 30 (getup_imit 5). The viewer's get-up from artifact v22. |
| aware.tss / .head | **go-to with a sense of where to step.** unified_3 trained on with the new look inputs (env.h OBS_LOOK: the path 0.5 and 2 m on, the ground at 16 points round her), progress paid only up to 1.2 m/s and heading fast for a drop costing the excess speed (gpu/pod_aware.sh, 75 min A40, 37M+ steps, hand-up 5%). tools/eval_nav.py, 256 x 30 s over the room bank with the 5% hand-up, against unified_3: goals 1042 (747), falls 187 (235), speed 1.13 m/s (1.39; 90th pct 1.58 against 2.14), on detours moving the path's way 85% (69%), at a drop ahead over 1.2 m/s 39% (47%). Without the hand-up it still falls within 2 s. In body.js over the slope gyms (48 x 30 s): goals 158 (106), falls 36 (45), at a drop over 1.2 m/s 23% (70%); with the viewer's steering point along the path, 10%. The viewer's Go to from artifact v25. |
| jump.tss / .head | jump courses, stage 1 (gpu/pod_jump.sh with CLIPS=all six, FROM=getup_track: 75 min A40, hand-up 0.3 adapting down): tracking the CMU jumps across gaps between platforms. It learned the landing, not the takeoff: started mid-flight it lands; from the run-up it falls short or crumples. Full jumps (0.5 s of run-up), standing on the far platform for a second after: 1 of 156 (the get-up tracker it started from: 0). The warm start for stage 2. |
| jump_standing.tss / .head | **jump courses, stage 2: the standing jumps.** jump trained on with the two standing forward jumps only (gpu/pod_jump.sh defaults: 75 min A40, starts mostly in the run-up, up to 1 m of drift, lying after a landing ends the episode, hand-up 0.2 adapting to 0). Full jumps from the run-up (0.9 m gaps, platforms 0.3 to 1.0 m up), standing on the far platform a second after: **145 of 205** (fjump1 79 of 117, fjump2 66 of 88; stage 1: 1 of 132). In body.js with the viewer's rules: 16 of 24; the viewer's course (jumps chained, each aimed the way her feet point) went 4 jumps three times out of three in a browser test. The viewer's Jump from artifact v26. |
| jump_stick.tss / .head | **jump courses, stage 3: sticking the landing.** jump_standing trained on with the landing paid for how far onto the platform the first foot comes down (to 0.3 m; gpu/pod_jump.sh FROM=jump_standing ASSIST=0.05, 75 min A40). Full jumps from the run-up landed and stood 195 of 203 (fjump1 112 of 116, fjump2 83 of 87; stage 2: 145 of 205). In body.js, each jump from its start nudged by 1e-4 m/s (as another browser's math would): fjump2 33 of 40 (stage 2: 12 of 24), fjump1 38 of 40 (2 of 24); all rooms and starts 107 of 128 stood (75). Chained in the viewer's course it averages 2 to 3 jumps: after a landing the direction of the next jump wanders (it never trained on one jump after another). The viewer's Jump from artifact v27. |
| chain_s1.tss / .head | jump_stick trained on chained jump courses (env.h TaskCfg.chain: every jump after the first from a real landing, aimed by her feet within 0.25 rad; gpu/pod_chain.sh stage 1, 45 min A40, hand-up adapted 5% -> 0.6%). tools/eval_chain.py, 128 x 30 s from each clip's start, the viewer's aim: 1.49 jumps in a row alone (jump_stick 1.19), 5.32 with a 0.6% hand-up (80% of episodes reaching 4, 64% reaching 6). The warm start for stage 2 (the hand-up taken away). |
