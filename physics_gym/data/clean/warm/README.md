# clean warm starts

Policies trained only on clean data (the CMU motion capture; no Bandai Namco clips), for continuing training.

| file | what |
|---|---|
| walk_cmu.tss / .head | **superseded: do not warm-start from it.** Trained on the CMU walk cycle as converted before the rest-direction fix in tools/cmu_to_ref.py, which crossed the legs (knees 0.27 m, feet 0.47 m past each other), and before leg-on-leg contact; it learned that crossed gait. Kept only as the record of the first clean run (unassisted, 64 x 20 s, no falls, 0.96 m/s). |
| walk_cmu2.tss / .head | **the clean walk prior.** Imitation of the corrected CMU walk cycle (legs no longer crossed), with leg-on-leg contact, CMU joint ranges and muscles: 90 min stage on an A40 from scratch, adaptive assist 0.5 -> 0 (this is the checkpoint at assist 0, about 70 min in). Unassisted, mean actions, 20 s on the open floor (drift stop off): 6% falls over 64 episodes (9% over 32), 0.98 m/s; knees 7.9 cm apart on average, crossed in 4% of steps (13% for the feet) against 31% and 53% for walk_cmu. |
| walk_cmu3.tss / .head | **the clean walk prior (current).** The same run as walk_cmu2, at its end (90 min, 81.5M steps, about 20 min of it unassisted). Unassisted, mean actions, 64 episodes of 20 s on the open floor (drift stop off): **no falls**, 0.98 m/s (0.97 forward); knees 7.9 cm apart on average, crossed in 1.7% of steps (feet 11%). |
| walk_robust_slick.tss / .head | walk_cmu3 trained on in free-heading imitation with pushes up to 80 N (40 min, gpu/pod_robust_terrain.sh), before static friction: 20 s, open floor, no falls with mean actions, 9% at sigma 0.14 (walk_cmu3: 98%). Under the static-friction physics (which grips where the old let feet slip) it lasts 10 s on average; the warm start for retraining on it. |
| walk_robust.tss / .head | walk_robust_slick retrained under static friction (free-heading imitation, pushes to 80 N, assist adapted to 0; 40 min, gpu/pod_robust_terrain.sh stage W): 20 s on open floor, no falls with mean actions, 5% at sigma 0.139, 70% at sigma 0.2. The viewer's Walk (CMU) from artifact v12. |
| walk_contact.tss / .head | walk_robust retrained after the contact fix (static friction per body against penetration depth, no bounce; gpu/pod_contact.sh stage W, 20 min, assist adapted to 0): 20 s open floor, no falls with mean actions, 2% at sigma 0.139. The viewer's Walk (CMU) from artifact v16. |
| goto_contact.tss / .head | go-to over the stage-1 room bank after the contact fix (gpu/pod_contact.sh stage A: from walk_contact, fresh goal/grip/probe inputs, goals ahead, 60% on raised surfaces; 40 min): 5.7% hand-up. The viewer's Go to from artifact v19; the unified run's warm start. |
| unified_3.tss / .head | the unified navigation run's last stage (gpu/pod_night.sh -> pod_unified.sh: 24 slope gyms + 8 stage-1 rooms, the path helper's waypoint as input and progress along the path as reward, 40 s of chained goals; 5 h). With its 5% hand-up: on the slope bank 74 goals in 228 s over 24 episodes (goto_contact: 34), but 19 of 24 fall within 15 s, a rushing gait (pelvis to 2.5 m/s) and hard foot strikes. Without the hand-up it falls within 2 s. The viewer's Go to from artifact v20. |
| getup_v2.tss / .head | get-up in the gym's physics (pod_night.sh step 1: CMU get-up clips as style, a hand-up fading 0.5 -> 0 over 40 min, then 20 min alone, entropy bonus): alone it reaches an upright kneel (pelvis 0.30 m, head 0.71 m) and stops; with a 20% hand-up it stands in about a second. The viewer's get-up (with that hand-up) from artifact v20. |
