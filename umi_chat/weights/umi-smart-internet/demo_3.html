explain photosynthesis as flashcards
Plants take sunlight, water, and CO2, and turn them into sugar and oxygen. You're welcome.
What are the two main stages of photosynthesis?
Light-dependent reactions (thylakoid membranes) split water, release O₂, and make ATP + NADPH.
Calvin cycle (stroma) uses that ATP/NADPH to fix CO₂ into glucose.
Overall: 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + light → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂
